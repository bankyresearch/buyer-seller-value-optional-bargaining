UNCERTAINTY CAVEAT
In the first complete analysis outputs, 13 of 144 pairwise cost-crossing ratio
diagnostics had more than 2.5% of bootstrap duration contrasts in each sign.
Ratios near a zero denominator can have unstable/disconnected uncertainty;
ordinary percentile endpoints do not establish a reliable switching boundary.
Two point crossings in the evaluated [0,.02] cost range were especially weak:
- Stubborn, acceptance .25, exogenous, responsive versus full FQI: c*=.002313,
  positive duration-denominator fraction .0465, negative .9530, undefined .0005.
- Stubborn, acceptance .50, value-dependent, responsive versus clock FQI:
  c*=.016674, positive fraction .8070, negative .1930.
These estimates must be retained as unstable diagnostics, not used as a
deployment prescription. The other sign flags and infeasible/undefined values
also remain visible. Cost ceilings use positive exposure H and are unaffected
by this signed policy-duration denominator issue. The analysis owner was asked
to make this reporting distinction explicit without deleting unfavorable cells.
Resolution: the final analysis now includes denominator_sign_instability and
describes it in analysis/schema.json; a sixteenth fixture test checks the flag.
Canonical and separate-directory tables were regenerated and verified by the
analysis owner. No computed point estimate was removed or selected afterward.

The 193 products, not 2,223,360 simulated policy trajectories, are bootstrap
clusters. Intervals condition on fixed seeds and stipulated customer/route
mechanisms. No adjustment for simulation structural uncertainty or measured
real-customer demand is implied. New trajectories do not restore an untouched
holdout after prior evaluation outcomes were inspected.
