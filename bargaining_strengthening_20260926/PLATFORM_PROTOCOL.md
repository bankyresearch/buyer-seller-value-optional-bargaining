# Synthetic bargaining and checkout choice control

Specified locally on 2026-09-26 before new outcome computation. This is exploratory simulation, not preregistration, observed customer adoption, independently specified external validation, or a causal business forecast.

Question: how does adding an optional bargaining route compare with checkout-only once full-price cannibalization, unsuccessful negotiation, fallback and interaction cost are included per original purchase opportunity?

Use the unchanged 193 evaluation products, 80 synthetic customer opportunities per product, and a new deterministic `platform-controls-20260926` random stream. Keep all existing private value, opening bid, buyer style, deadline, abandonment, price-floor proxy and cent-rounding definitions. Compare the already development-selected Time concession and Buyer responsive rules without retuning. Cross three regimes (Reference, Stubborn offers, Low valuations) and customer acceptance coefficient a in {0, .25, .5, 1}, where quote acceptance requires ask <= bid + a(value-bid) and ask <= value. Seller acceptance of qualifying bids remains unchanged. Parameter changes are synthetic assumption sensitivity, not empirically estimated behavior.

Modes: checkout-only; forced negotiation (everyone negotiates and unsuccessful negotiations do not fall back); choice-enabled (non-opt-ins have ordinary checkout; opt-ins negotiate and can fall back after failure). For choice-enabled, cross base opt-in probability p in {.25,.5,.75}, fallback probability r in {0,.5,1}, and per-round cost c in {0,.005,.02} listing units. Every mode also has the stated cost grid, except checkout-only has zero incremental negotiation cost. Costs apply to both the buyer and seller separately at the same illustrative rate. They represent two distinct resource/effort burdens; joint net surplus subtracts both. They are incurred on every attempted negotiation round, including failed episodes, and do not change the simulated acceptance decisions. This is an accounting sensitivity, not a forward-looking endogenous choice equilibrium.

Primary opt-in mechanism is exogenous p independent of customer value. Sensitivity has value-dependent odds: multiply p/(1-p) by 2 below listing and by 0.5 at/above listing, then transform back to probability. This is synthetic private customer self-selection, not a seller using private values. Label p as a base probability, and report effective opt-in probability. Neither mechanism asserts rational or measured customer choice. Integrate opt-in/fallback probabilities analytically conditional on each potential negotiation trajectory; do not count each probability mixture as additional observed customers or independent trajectories. Fallback retains only customers with value >= listing and probability r after a failed negotiation, including synthetic abandonment/deadline exits. Thus r is an aggregate re-engagement assumption, not an immediate post-deadline action assumption.

Per-opportunity gross seller surplus is (1-q)S_checkout + q[S_negotiation + (1-D_negotiation)rS_checkout]. Deduct c*q*negotiation_rounds for net seller surplus. Apply the analogous buyer equation and cost, with ordinary checkout buyer surplus max(value-1,0). Forced bargaining uses q=1,r=0; checkout-only uses q=0. A purchase can occur through exactly one route; no double counting. Report agreement, seller and buyer gross/net surplus, rounds, new-sales margin below listing, foregone full-price discount margin, lost checkout margin after unsuccessful non-fallback, and a reconciled paired incremental seller surplus versus checkout. Full-price discounts count as cannibalization only for customers who would buy at listing (value >= 1), rather than treating every discount as lost revenue.

Average over 80 opportunities within each product. Use 2,000 product bootstrap draws and paired contrasts, conditional on the simulator. Report the complete factorial grid, no best-test configuration selection, and no p-value winner claims. Main illustrative configuration is predefined p=.5,r=.5,c=.005,exogenous selection,a=.25, with other combinations retained as sensitivity. Store source hashes, every base scenario and negotiation trajectory, compact counterfactual sufficient statistics, per-product outcomes, all summaries and assertions. The compact per-opportunity factors plus the fully specified parameter grid are the exact reconstruction inputs for all probability-integrated outcomes; do not duplicate millions of identical identifiers into large CSV files.
