## Factors and fixed resources

Use the existing 555 training, 182 development and 193 evaluation products, previously processed inputs and `revision-v1` matched scenario generator. Training seeds are 271, 811, 1601, 2027 and 4093. Training customer streams remain `training-SEED`; reference development uses 40 episodes/product and the unchanged `development` stream. Final evaluation uses 80 episodes/product and the unchanged `evaluation` stream.

At 32 training episodes per product, fit the full public-state FQI policy in four cells: uniform exploration with nine actions; mixed exploration with nine actions; uniform exploration with enriched actions; mixed exploration with enriched actions. Uniform exploration draws independently from all available actions at each live state. The nine actions are fractions 0, .125, ..., 1 of the remaining previous-ask-to-floor gap. Enriched actions add the exact state-specific fraction that reaches the clipped linear concession schedule, `1 - (1 - floor) * round / horizon`; the corresponding quote is computed with double-precision merchant inputs and normal cent rounding. The enriched candidate is available during exploration, Bellman backup and deployment. It includes an exact implementation of the selected reference linear schedule rather than requiring the learner to approximate it with the nine fixed fractions.

Mixed exploration balances four trajectory components within each product using `((episode_index // 4) + (episode_index % 4)) % 4`: uniform random actions; a slow .5-terminal linear schedule; the 1.0-terminal linear schedule; and a fixed ask. This assignment is orthogonal to the simulator's `episode_index % 4` customer-style assignment: every style receives every exploration component equally at both training budgets. At each live state it follows that component with probability .75 and draws a uniform random action with probability .25. Under the nine-action set, a schedule is projected to the nearest available fraction, with lower index breaking ties; under enrichment, the full linear schedule is exact. This is a deliberate training-coverage intervention, not evidence of customer realism.

Also fit the mixed/enriched full-state policy at 128 episodes/product, and a clock-only ablation on the exact same 128-episode transition arrays. This yields six specification/mode combinations, five seeds, and two candidate minimum-leaf sizes, or 60 fitted candidates. Each uses 24 ExtraTrees, maximum depth 12, eight fitted-Q iterations, undiscounted terminal seller surplus, and minimum-leaf candidates 40 and 160. Select one leaf separately per combination by mean development-product seller surplus averaged equally over all five seeds. Do not choose a best test seed, stress-specific hyperparameter, or best evaluation specification. The full 128 mixed/enriched design is the prespecified strengthened learner, not a data-selected winner among the controls.

Only public state enters the seller: round fraction, horizon, rounded merchant floor, current buyer offer, previous seller ask, previous buyer offer, bid change, running bid minimum and running bid maximum. Clock-only keeps round fraction, horizon, floor and previous ask. Private value, deadline, customer type and future random draws are not features. The state remains an approximate history summary, not a proved Markov state.

Primary reference contrast: strengthened full-state FQI minus the time-concession rule, seller surplus. Control contrasts are mixed minus uniform at matched action sets, enriched minus nine actions at matched exploration, 128 minus 32 episodes for the mixed/enriched full policy, and full minus clock at 128. Report them as specification checks, not identified causes beyond the controlled computational interventions.

Report transition counts, per-round training state coverage near the linear rule (previous ask within .02 of its round-start quote), action representability, training elapsed time and checkpoint sizes. ## Pre-evaluation correction

During concurrent code review, after ten uniform/grid candidates and seven mixed/grid development candidates had completed but before any new evaluation outcomes ran, the initial `episode_index % 4` mixture assignment was found to equal the simulator's customer-style assignment. This would confound behavior-policy mixture with customer style. The job was interrupted, the seven mixed candidate scores/checkpoints and four mixed transition datasets were preserved in `learning_results/superseded_mixture_assignment`, and the orthogonal assignment above was specified. Those superseded fits are diagnostic artifacts, not part of any reported outcome or selection. Uniform results are unaffected. All mixed candidates are regenerated. The original specification is preserved in the diagnostic archive. This correction was motivated by code inspection, not evaluation performance.
